# United States (`US`)

## Basics

- **Exchanges**: NYSE and Nasdaq. Write a stock as its ticker (`AAPL`, `JPM`, `BRK.B`).
- **Hours**: 09:30–16:00 ET, Monday to Friday. Pre-market from 04:00 ET, after-hours to 20:00 ET. Time zone `America/New_York`.
- **Holidays**: look up the NYSE holiday calendar for the current year (early closes too).
- **Default run times**: morning 08:00 ET (before the open, after most pre-market earnings), evening 17:00 ET (after the close and most after-hours earnings).
- **Fiscal year**: varies by company (Apple ends in September, Microsoft in June, Walmart in January). Always label quarters with the company's own fiscal year and say so ("FQ1 2027, quarter ending Dec 2026").

## Numbers

- Currency USD, locale `en-US`. Company figures in $M or $B, consistent within a table. Per-share figures in $ with two decimals.
- GAAP vs non-GAAP (adjusted) figures: say which. Lead with GAAP when they differ a lot.

## Market snapshot

- **Indices**: S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite, Dow Jones Industrial Average, Russell 2000, VIX.
- **Before the open**: S&P 500 and Nasdaq futures, overnight Asia and Europe.
- **Rates and macro**: 10-year Treasury yield, US dollar index (DXY), WTI and Brent crude, gold.
- **Sectors**: add sector moves (the S&P sector ETFs) when notable or relevant to a holding.
- **Calendar**: FOMC decisions and minutes, CPI, PCE, the monthly jobs report (usually first Friday), GDP, retail sales, earnings dates for holdings.

## Where to look

1. **SEC EDGAR** (primary): 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, proxy (DEF 14A), Form 4 insider trades, 13D/13G stakes, S-1/S-3 offerings.
2. **Company investor relations**: earnings releases, presentations, call webcasts and transcripts.
3. **Data, free pages**: Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Macrotrends, Stock Analysis, FRED (macro), BLS, the Federal Reserve.
4. **News**: Reuters, AP, CNBC, MarketWatch, Barron's and WSJ and Bloomberg and FT (headlines; most are paywalled, so don't summarise beyond what's visible).
5. **Paid research**: only if the person shared it.

Prices from web search can be delayed. Write "as of <time ET>" beside any price.

## Red flags to check (US)

- **8-K Item 4.01** (auditor change) or **4.02** (prior financials can't be relied on) [high]
- Late filing notice (**NT 10-K / NT 10-Q**) [high]
- **Going concern** language, covenant breach, or waiver [high]
- **Restatement** or material weakness in internal controls [high]
- Cluster of **insider selling** on Form 4 outside 10b5-1 plans, or CEO/CFO selling a large share of holdings [medium]
- **CFO or auditor exit** without a clear reason [medium]
- **SEC, DOJ or FTC** investigation, subpoena or Wells notice [high]
- **Short-seller report** published [medium; high if specific allegations]
- **Dilution**: new shelf (S-3) used heavily, at-the-market programmes, large stock-based compensation relative to free cash flow [medium]
- Guidance cut or withdrawn [medium]
- Accounts receivable or inventory growing much faster than revenue; operating cash flow well below net income for 2+ years [medium]
- Debt maturities in the next 18 months that look hard to refinance [medium]
- Credit rating downgrade by S&P, Moody's or Fitch [medium]

## Valuation defaults

- **Risk-free rate**: 10-year Treasury yield. Look up.
- **Equity risk premium**: look up the current implied ERP (Damodaran publishes it monthly).
- **Terminal growth** (nominal, USD): base 2.5%, range 2–3%. Never above the risk-free rate.
- **Tax rate**: 21% federal plus state where material; use the company's sustainable effective rate.
- **Multiples**: the company's own history and US listed peers.

## Language

Use standard US terms: earnings call, guidance, EPS, free cash flow, buybacks, insiders.

## Compliance note

Everything the tracker writes is personal research notes, not investment advice. Say "watch", "review", "worth a closer look", never "buy", "sell" or a price target presented as a call.
