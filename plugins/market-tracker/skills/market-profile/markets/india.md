# India (`IN`)

## Basics

- **Exchanges**: NSE (primary for liquidity) and BSE. Write a stock as its NSE symbol (`TATAPOWER`, `HDFCBANK`, `M&M`). Use the 6-digit BSE code (`500400`) only for BSE-only stocks.
- **Hours**: pre-open 09:00–09:15 IST, normal trading 09:15–15:30 IST, Monday to Friday. Time zone `Asia/Kolkata`.
- **Holidays**: look up the NSE trading holiday list for the current year. Diwali has a one-hour Muhurat trading session; its timing is announced each year.
- **Default run times**: morning 08:00 IST (before pre-open), evening 18:00 IST (after the close and usually after provisional FII/DII data).
- **Fiscal year**: April to March. FY27 = 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Quarters are Q1 Apr–Jun, Q2 Jul–Sep, Q3 Oct–Dec, Q4 Jan–Mar. Label results "Q2 FY27", never "Q3 2026".
- **Results timing**: quarterly results within 45 days of quarter end, Q4 and full year within 60 days. So results season runs roughly mid-July to mid-August, mid-October to mid-November, mid-January to mid-February, and April to end of May.

## Numbers

- Currency INR, locale `en-IN`. Use Indian grouping: ₹1,23,456.
- Company figures (revenue, profit, debt, market cap) in **₹ crore** (1 crore = 10 million = 100 lakh). Small amounts in ₹ lakh. Never mix crore and million in one table.
- Per-share figures in ₹ with two decimals.
- Say whether figures are consolidated or standalone. Prefer consolidated.

## Market snapshot (what goes in every brief)

- **Indices**: Nifty 50, Sensex, Nifty Bank, Nifty Midcap 100, Nifty Smallcap 100, India VIX. Add sector indices (Nifty IT, Auto, Pharma, PSU Bank, Metal, Realty, FMCG, Energy) only when they moved notably or matter to a holding.
- **Before the open**: GIFT Nifty (trades on NSE IX) as the pre-market indicator, overnight US close, Asian markets at the time of writing.
- **Flows**: FII (FPI) and DII net buying or selling in the cash market, provisional figures published by NSE after the close, in ₹ crore. Month-to-date totals help. If the day's figures aren't out by the evening run, write "FII/DII figures not yet published" and include them next morning.
- **Macro**: INR per USD, Brent crude (India imports most of its oil, so crude moves matter), gold, the India 10-year G-sec yield.
- **Calendar**: RBI Monetary Policy Committee decisions (six a year), CPI inflation (around the 12th of each month), IIP, GDP (quarterly), GST collections and auto sales (start of each month), the Union Budget (1 February), and major global events (US Fed decisions, US CPI).

## Where to look

Search in roughly this order and cite what you use:

1. **Exchange filings** (the primary source): NSE and BSE corporate announcements for results, board meetings, investor presentations, concall transcripts and audio links, credit rating changes, auditor changes, insider and promoter trades (SAST and PIT disclosures), pledges, bulk and block deals.
2. **Company investor relations pages**: annual reports, presentations, transcripts.
3. **Financial data, free pages**: Screener.in (10-year financials, ratios, shareholding, links to concalls), Trendlyne, Tickertape, Moneycontrol.
4. **News**: The Economic Times (Markets), Mint, Business Standard, Financial Express, Moneycontrol, The Hindu BusinessLine, CNBC-TV18, NDTV Profit.
5. **Regulators and data**: SEBI orders and press releases, RBI, MOSPI (CPI, IIP, GDP), PIB.
6. **Paid research** (Equitymaster, Value Research, broker reports): only if the person has shared the report or its text. Don't summarise a paywalled report from its headline.

Prices from web search can be 15 minutes or more delayed. Write "as of <time IST>" next to any price you use and take index levels from two sources when they disagree.

## Red flags to check (India)

Check these for every holding and watched company. Severity in brackets is the default; raise it when the size is large.

- Promoter **pledge** created or increased [high], or invoked by lenders [high]
- Promoter stake **sold** in the open market or reduced quarter on quarter [medium; high if large]
- **Auditor** resignation, qualified opinion, or emphasis of matter [high]
- Delay in filing results, or results without a limited review [high]
- **CFO, CEO or company secretary exit** without a clear reason [medium]
- Large or rising **related-party transactions**, loans to promoter group companies [medium]
- **Credit rating** downgrade or watch-negative by CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings [high]
- **SEBI, ED, CBI, GST or income tax** action, show-cause notice or order [high]
- Stock put in **ASM or GSM** surveillance framework, or in the F&O ban list [medium]
- **Dilution**: QIP, preferential issue at a discount, large ESOP grants, warrants to promoters [medium]
- Receivable days or inventory days rising faster than sales; operating cash flow far below profit for 2+ years [medium]
- Contingent liabilities large relative to net worth [medium]
- Frequent changes in accounting policy or financial year [medium]
- Guidance cut in a concall, or management avoiding questions they answered before [medium]
- Bulk or block deals by known operators or promoter entities [info]

## Valuation defaults

Start here and adjust per company, saying why:

- **Risk-free rate**: India 10-year G-sec yield. Look up.
- **Equity risk premium**: mature-market premium plus India's country risk premium. Look up the latest Damodaran country ERP table.
- **Terminal growth** (nominal, INR): base 5%, range 4–6%. Never above the risk-free rate.
- **Tax rate**: 25.17% for companies on the new regime (Section 115BAA); use the company's actual effective rate if it differs and it's sustainable.
- **Multiples**: compare with the company's own 5- and 10-year history and with Indian listed peers. Don't compare to US multiples.
- Per-share value in ₹, totals in ₹ crore.

## Language

Indian investors talk about promoters (founding shareholders), concalls (earnings calls), FIIs and DIIs, OPM (operating profit margin), and PAT (profit after tax). Use these terms.

## Compliance note

Investment advice in India needs SEBI registration. Everything the tracker writes is personal research notes: say "watch", "review", "worth a closer look", never "buy", "sell" or a target price presented as a call.
